About AMRIT

Atma Malik Institute of Technology & research (AMRIT) is a premier educational institution established in 2010 under Jangali Maharaj Ashram Trust. Located in Kokamthan, Maharashtra, AMRIT has emerged as a center of excellence in engineering education.

Academic Excellence

AICTE approved programs with experienced faculty and modern teaching methodologies.

State-of-the-art Infrastructure

Well-equipped laboratories, digital library, and modern amenities for hands-on learning.

Industry Connect
