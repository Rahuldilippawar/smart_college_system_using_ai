Library

To promote a spirit of continuous learning among its students the Atma Malik Institute of Technology & Research assures peaceful and enriching hours in the library. It has about 5000+ volumes and 2500+ titles of books. In addition to which, the library also subscribes to International and National journals and periodicals.

In this fully automated library, students and staff members can search for books by author, title or subject heading and their availability. Each student is given 2 library books at a time and they have allocated library hours/week.

  • Excellent reading halls that has reference books, hand books, dictionaries and encyclopedias
  • All the books and periodicals are arranged department wise
  • Maintains back issues of volumes of the periodicals, previous year question papers, and project reports, which are organized department wise in a chronological order
  • All students and staff of the college are members of the Library

To access e-books: Web OPAC System
